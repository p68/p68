        Ladder of the month

        The mistake: a dropped stitch three rows below, on the plain stockinette back of a DK jumper. We have all been here.

        1. Stop. Put the knitting down. Step away from the cardigan.
        2. Catch the live stitch on a spare needle or a safety pin. Do not breathe on it.
        3. Find the loose strand ladder running up. Count the rungs. That's how many rows you get to climb.
        4. With a crochet hook, pull each rung through the loop, front to back, like a very small lift.
        5. Slip it back on the left needle, untwisted. Pretend it never happened. Tell everyone it happened.
