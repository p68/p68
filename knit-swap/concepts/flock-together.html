Yarn Fair Returns to St Aidan's!

After last year's unfortunate business with the urn, we are delighted to say the Harvest Yarn & Needle Fair will go ahead on Saturday 14 November, 10 till 3, in the church hall. Tables are £4 (members) or £7 (non-members, you cheeky lot). Please label your dye lots! Last year somebody sold a "Heather Mix" that turned out to be three different heathers and a Mrs Pickersgill had words.

This app is the secretary's own idea, built over a long weekend with a library book and a lot of tea. If something looks wonky, it is meant to. Ring Dot (the Sec.) on 01423 555 0147 with any comments.

Minutes of the meeting held Thursday 1 October

  1. Present: 23 members and Mr Rudd's dog. Apologies: Hilda G. (hip), Bev T. (lambing, early).
  2. Minutes of last meeting read and agreed, apart from the spelling of "Fair Isle".
  3. Treasurer reported £212.40 in the tin and £38.15 at the bank.
  4. The Charity Blanket is now 61 squares of a planned 80. Squares to Olive by the 5th, please. Garter stitch is fine. It is always fine.
  5. Proposed (Joan A.) and seconded (Stanley R.) that tea be 50p. Carried, with one against.
  6. AOB: who has the key to the cupboard? Nobody. Meeting closed 9.12 pm.

Subs Are Due

Annual subs of £12 fall due on 1 November. Pay Colin in cash, by cheque, or in a skein of something nice (he'll value it fairly). New members can join right here in this very newsletter.

Swap & Sell — Classifieds

Leftovers, spares and finished bits from your fellow members. Prices are £ and honest. "Swap?" means the member would sooner trade than take cash. Contact details are at the hall or via the Sec.

Dye lots! Always check the lot number before buying more than one ball. Different lots can differ a good deal, even when the label says the same colour.
