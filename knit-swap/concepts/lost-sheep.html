How the flock works

  1. Find a stray.
    Browse by weight, fibre, or whether it wants swapping or selling.
  2. Foster it.
    It goes in your basket and the crofter keeps it back for three days.
  3. Meet on the track.
    Check the map for who's nearest, and when their gate is open.
